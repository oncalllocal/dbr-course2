# Statistics research (2026-11-04) and what we may say

**Verdict: no single authoritative study of missed calls at home-service companies.** Published figures come mostly from vendors selling answering tools, and the same numbers get repeated without a checkable origin. Do not put any third-party statistic on screen unless the original source has been opened and read.

| Claim seen | What the search found | Safe to use? |
|---|---|---|
| Home-service missed-call rate | Ranges from 14% (CallRail, Jan 2025) to 27% and 48% (Invoca) to 74% (a vendor's own 45-contractor sample). Methods differ. | No single number. "Studies range widely" at most. |
| "62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered" | Traces to a January 2016 study of 85 businesses in 58 industries, not home services. | No |
| "80-86% of callers don't leave a voicemail" / "62-67% call a competitor" / "85% never call back" | Vendor blogs; CallRail's survey says 42% of consumers do leave a voicemail. Sources conflict. | No |
| "98% SMS open rate" | Attribution unclear, may be disowned by its original source. | No |
| "SMS 45% response vs email 6%" | Traces to Business.com, unverified. | No |
| Speed-to-lead (HBR 2011, Oldroyd et al.; MIT/InsideSales) | Real, widely cited, but figures differ by summary (7x and 21x). Original article not read. | Only after reading the original HBR article |

## What we CAN use (first party, true)
1. **Our own audit results.** After tonight's run we will know how many of the businesses we called after hours did not answer. "We called N [trade] companies in Tampa after hours; X did not pick up" is real, current and specific. Needs a clear method note and a sample size.
2. **The case study:** the unnamed $12M/yr home-service company: 24/7 human answering plus missed-call text back as backup; $83,000 in business converted from the backup this year (owner-supplied, permission given).
3. **The owner's job values:** $12,000 full repipe, $15,000 HVAC system replacement.
