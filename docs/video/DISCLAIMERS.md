## Open items for the owner
1. **Substantiation file.** Keep records behind every objective claim: the 38% (calls, dates, method), the $83K+ (invoices or job records), the client's written permission to use the figure (the client is not named), and the date of that permission.
2. **Material connection.** The video says the result is from a current client. If they receive anything of value for appearing, say so.
3. **38% wording.** Say exactly what was measured: after-hours calls we placed to businesses listing 24/7 hours. Do not describe it as a statistic about all home service businesses.
4. **Usage fees.** Texts, calls and AI are billed separately. The video no longer mentions them (owner decision 2026-11-04); they must be stated clearly on the live call and in writing before the customer pays.
5. **AI disclosure.** The video says the assistant identifies itself as a virtual assistant. Confirm the bot is actually configured that way, or remove the line. Some states require disclosure when a bot texts consumers.
6. **Guarantee.** None is stated. If you add "we keep working free until the first captured lead", state the conditions (forwarding active, etc.).
7. **Emails (CAN-SPAM).** Accurate sender and subject, a physical postal address, a working unsubscribe link honored within 10 days. "We called last night at [time]" must be true for that recipient.
8. **Voice.** The narrator is an AI voice (ElevenLabs, Lauren B). Do not present it as a named real person. Confirm your ElevenLabs plan permits commercial use.
9. **Business calls.** The prerecorded audit call identifies the company name only (owner decision). Review with counsel whether it needs more.
