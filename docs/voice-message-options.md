"Hi, this is OnCall Local calling to check after-hours phone availability for local service businesses. No action is needed on your part. Thank you, and have a good evening."

**Owner decision (2026-11-03):** no callback number is voiced. The message identifies the company by name only.

## Other options considered

The message must identify who is calling and give a callback number, and what it says must be true.
Calling it a "test" while you are actually prospecting can read as deceptive, so say what it is.

**A (neutral, recommended)**
"Hi, this is [Your Company] calling to check after-hours phone availability for local service businesses. No action is needed on your part. If you'd like details, call us at [number]. Thank you, and have a good evening."

**B (short)**
"Hello, this is [Your Company]. We're running a quick after-hours call check. No action needed. Questions? Call [number]. Thanks."

**C (curiosity)**
"Hi, this is [Your Company]. We're checking whether local contractors' phones are answered after hours. You'll get a short note from us tomorrow with what we found. No action needed. Our number is [number]."

C works best with the next-day email, because the email delivers what the call promised.
